## Educational illustrations and texture

The village, woodblock, brush, and gallery objects are contemporary illustrations. The process story's inline SVG is a simplified contemporary Mouse Wedding study, with separate red, green, white, yellow, and black groups. It is not a recovered block set or a reproduction of the photographed museum print. The existing workshop studies and simulation are unchanged.

`paper-fibers.svg` is an original, subtle vector texture suggesting paper fibers, not a photograph or scan of historical dó paper. Interface pigment colors are screen approximations.
